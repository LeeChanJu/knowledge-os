### Why this responsibility exists

Create deterministic evidence boundaries that remain attributable to a DocumentVersion.

### Inputs and outputs

Input: Extracted text and declared chunk_size.

Output: Ordered text chunks.

### What must be preserved

- No separate service or store.
- Do not lose evidence to satisfy a size bound. F6 records that long paragraphs exceed the intended hard limit.

### Follow the example

A very long Korean paragraph must not lose its ending just to fit a bound. Check both text preservation and the length of the resulting passages.

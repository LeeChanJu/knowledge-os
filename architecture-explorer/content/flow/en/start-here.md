## Why not let AI decide on its own?

```diagram
📄 “We reviewed Joule Studio and A2A.”
🤖 “Joule Studio uses A2A!” → stronger than the evidence ❌
🧑 Read the original → reject or correct the suggestion
```

Two names appearing together does not establish a usage relationship. AI output therefore enters as a [candidate for review](term:proposal) and needs [review and acceptance](term:approval). This fictional “SAP AI study note” is our example throughout all four lessons, not evidence of product capabilities.

## Who turns the material into an answer?

```diagram
“Find my saved notes about SAP A2A.”
Knowledge OS → returns readable notes and supporting material
Claude/GPT → consults the material → answers me
```

Knowledge OS returns material and connections so an answer can be checked. Claude/GPT writes the answer; it must not invent a plausible relationship when the material does not support it.

## How does today's implementation differ from the goal?

There are [connection tools](term:mcp) for bounded reads and proposals. An independently authorized reviewer approves outside those tools. Automated extraction providers and production meaning-search population remain intentionally deferred.

[Accepted knowledge](term:canonical) can still be wrong. Review history and original evidence let us revisit and correct it. The map describes connected responsibilities, not proof that every path has completed verification.

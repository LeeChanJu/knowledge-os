Combining complementary retrieval signals to find useful evidence.

## The problem at this stage

A name index and a similarity map can help in different ways. Different queries need different signals; no single retrieval method is always best.

## Follow one study note

Remembering the word “Joule” can locate a candidate with keyword search. Receiving a result does not establish that its passage answers the question.

## Distinguish the concept from its implementation

Do not depict vector, full-text and graph as one automatically fused three-way runtime pipeline. Provider inactivity and evaluation limits remain visible.

Hybrid retrieval combines keyword and semantic results; graph traversal remains a distinct available contract path.

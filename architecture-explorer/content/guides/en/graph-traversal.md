Following allowed relationships from one known thing to another.

## The problem at this stage

Start at a person and follow a connection to the project they work on. Relationships answer connected questions rather than only locating similar text.

## Follow one study note

Follow accepted relations to read neighbors. The graph does not automatically invent and persist relationships that were never recorded.

## Distinguish the concept from its implementation

Accessible entity counts distinguish graph support from actual authorized data readiness. This is not unlimited Cypher access.

Graph reads are bounded and enforce workspace and evidence authorization.

## Boundaries the implementation must preserve

- Known IDs do not bypass ACLs. Canonical reads require all supporting evidence to remain accessible.

## Inputs, outputs and data responsibility

- Entity/record IDs or discovery query, access context, optional as-of time.
- Bounded authorized canonical records and traced discovery results.
- No separate database; retrieval traces belong to the operations store.

Turning source passages into candidate structured statements. It is one part of the larger document journey. Structure is useful, but generated candidates must not be accepted without governance.

## Follow one study note

A statement drafted from the note enters as an evidence-backed candidate. Resubmission must not erase rejection history or manufacture another approval.
